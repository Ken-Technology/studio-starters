How it works

Connect your point of sale, import your team, and run payroll in minutes. Tips are pooled and split by the rules you set, and every employee gets paid on time.

From point of sale to payday in three steps

What a payroll week looks like once Juniper is connected to the tills.4

  • Connect the point of sale5

    Hours and tips come in from the tills at each location, so nothing is keyed in twice.

  • Set the tip rules

    Pool and split tips by the rules you set once, and Juniper applies them every week.

  • Run payroll weekly

    Every employee at every location is paid on time, every week.

  1. 4Section intro. Frames the section as a week in the life, so an operations lead reads it as their own routine.
  2. 5The three points. The three steps keep your order and get a line each on what happens at the till, in the rules and on payday.
