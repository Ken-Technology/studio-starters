We offer a comprehensive range of services to meet your needs, including kitting, vendor-managed inventory, and custom sourcing. Our experienced team is committed to providing exceptional service.

Three ways Marlow keeps your line running

Beyond the catalogue, Marlow does the work that keeps parts on the shelf before you need them.

  • Kitting5

    Every fastener for one assembly in one bag, so the build starts without a parts hunt.

  • Vendor-managed inventory

    Marlow keeps your bins stocked, so maintenance never waits on a missing bolt.

  • Custom sourcing

    The part that is not in the catalogue, found and quoted by the same team that stocks the rest.
