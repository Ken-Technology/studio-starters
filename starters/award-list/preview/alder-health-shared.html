How the list was made

  1. Same industry as Alder Health PartnersCompanies whose profile lists hospitals and health care.
  2. A hiring surgeAt least 5 net new people in sales, engineering or operations over the last 6 months.
  3. Headcount growthHeadcount up 10 percent or more at the latest count.
  4. Ranked by headcountThe search returns up to 40 companies that pass both. Those are ordered by headcount, largest first, and the list shows the top 24.
  5. Alder Health PartnersPlaced by its size band, 1,001 to 5,000 people, since this page holds an exact headcount for every company but yours.

No awards body is behind this list. It is Larkspur's own, built by Priya Raman from company profiles and hiring data, and the filters above are the whole method.
