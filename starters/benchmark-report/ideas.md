# Ideas
## 1. Industry-wide benchmark
Starts from: benchmark-report
Fills: `data.benchmarks`, `data.focus`, `data.chart`
Why it gets a reply: a small account sees the whole industry and where the leaders sit.
Drop the size band filter from the peers step and raise the limit, so every account sees its industry as one group.
## 2. Quarterly benchmark refresh
Starts from: benchmark-report
Fills: `data.headline`, `data.summary`, `data.takeaways`
Why it gets a reply: new numbers are a reason to write again to someone who went quiet.
Upload the new quarter's CSV and have the summary say what moved since last time.
## 3. Benchmark for one function
Starts from: benchmark-report
Fills: `data.benchmarks`, `data.observations`, `data.next_steps`
Why it gets a reply: it speaks to one team lead about the numbers they own.
Keep only the sales or support metrics in the CSV and word the next steps for that leader.
