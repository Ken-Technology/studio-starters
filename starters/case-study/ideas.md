# Ideas
## 1. Story by region
Starts from: case-study
Fills: `data.stories`, `data.parallel`, `data.fit`
Why it gets a reply: a customer in their own region is easier to call and check.
Add a region column to the CSV and a second filter on the same-industry step, so the story is local first.
## 2. Story for a lost deal
Starts from: case-study
Fills: `data.headline`, `data.before`, `data.result`
Why it gets a reply: it answers the objection that stopped the deal with a customer who had it.
Pick stories by the problem column instead of industry and open the headline on the objection.
## 3. Two stories side by side
Starts from: case-study
Fills: `data.stories`, `data.customer`, `data.result`
Why it gets a reply: two results from similar companies are harder to dismiss than one.
Let the prompt pick two rows and repeat the story and result blocks, one under the other.
