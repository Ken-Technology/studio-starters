# Ideas
## 1. Vendor switch checklist
Starts from: checklist-doc
Fills: `data.title`, `data.checks`, `data.offer`
Why it gets a reply: an account weighing a switch gets the questions to ask before it talks to anyone.
Tell the prompt the decision is switching your category of tool, and keep the checks neutral about your product.
## 2. Onboarding checklist for new hires
Starts from: checklist-doc
Fills: `data.title`, `data.intro`, `data.checks`
Why it gets a reply: a team that is hiring will print it for the first week.
Point the decision at onboarding a new person in the account's industry, from the hiring news in the dossier.
## 3. Compliance prep checklist
Starts from: checklist-doc
Fills: `data.dossier`, `data.checks`, `data.closing`
Why it gets a reply: a deadline on the dossier makes it urgent to pin up.
Build the checks around one rule or audit named in the dossier and offer to go through the open ones.
