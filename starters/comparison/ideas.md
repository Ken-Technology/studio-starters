# Ideas
## 1. Switch from spreadsheets
Starts from: comparison
Fills: `data.catalog`, `data.current_tool`, `data.us_fit`
Why it gets a reply: most small accounts use no tool, and this meets them where they are.
Fill the CSV with one competitor named spreadsheets and a whiteboard, and aim the page at accounts with no tool on their site.
## 2. Renewal-window comparison
Starts from: comparison
Fills: `data.headline`, `data.intro`, `data.rows`
Why it gets a reply: it lands when they are deciding whether to renew.
Send it to accounts known to use one competitor and make the intro about the decision before their next renewal.
## 3. Head-to-head with one rival
Starts from: comparison
Fills: `data.catalog`, `data.rows`, `data.them_fit`
Why it gets a reply: a page about the one tool they already pay for is hard to ignore.
Load the CSV with a single competitor, so every account in a campaign sees the same side-by-side, tuned to their size.
