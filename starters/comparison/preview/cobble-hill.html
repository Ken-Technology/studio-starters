RouteboardThe rowTidewell

  1. Dispatch board

    Matters most when the day changes after 8 am.

    Better fit: Tidewell

    Routeboard

    A list-style board with a map view. Moving a job means editing it, and the technician sees it on the next sync.

    Tidewell

    One board per day with crews as rows. Drag a job to a crew and the technician's phone updates on the spot.

  2. Technician app

    Ask your technicians how often the app spins.

    Better fit: Tidewell

    Routeboard

    Needs a connection for job details and photos, which bites in basements and rural areas.

    Tidewell

    Works without signal. Checklists, photos and the customer's signature sync when the phone is back online.

  3. Online booking

    If most jobs come in through your website, this row counts double.

    Better fit: Routeboard

    Routeboard

    A booking widget that sits on your site and shows live availability. Customers already know it.

    Tidewell

    A booking page with your services and time windows. No branded widget for your own site yet.

  4. Invoicing

    About the same

    Routeboard

    Invoice from the job, card on file, financing offers built in.

    Tidewell

    Invoice from the job, card on file, deposit links.

  5. Reporting

    A plus if someone on your team lives in reports.

    Better fit: Routeboard

    Routeboard

    A report builder you shape yourself, plus a dashboard per branch.

    Tidewell

    Twelve standard reports and a CSV export for anything else.

  6. Accounting sync

    Better fit: Tidewell

    Routeboard

    One-way export, run by hand, with a reconcile step at month end.

    Tidewell

    Two-way sync with the common accounting tools. Invoices and payments post the same day.

  7. Getting set up

    Routeboard's longer setup also covers more custom work.

    Better fit: Tidewell

    Routeboard

    Tidewell

Where Routeboard is the better fit

Stay with Routeboard if most of your jobs come in through the booking widget on your site, or if someone on your team builds their own reports and would miss the report builder and the per-branch dashboard.

  • Reporting

Where Tidewell is the better fit

Tidewell is the better fit if your day changes after 8 am and you want the board and the technician's phone to move together, if your technicians lose signal in basements, and if you want to be live in two weeks rather than six to eight.

  • Dispatch board
  • Technician app
  • Accounting sync
  • Getting set up
