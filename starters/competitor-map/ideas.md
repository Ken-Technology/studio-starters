# Ideas
## 1. Win-loss conversation opener
Starts from: competitor-map
Fills: `data.competitors`, `data.open_space`, `data.headline`
Why it gets a reply: it asks the one thing the buyer knows better than you, who they lose deals to.
Keep the map and change the last section to ask which two of the placed companies they meet most in deals.
## 2. Investor view of a market
Starts from: competitor-map
Fills: `data.market`, `data.axes`, `data.account_position`
Why it gets a reply: a fund sees a category laid out on two axes in one page.
Point the page at a firm or fund instead of a company, and have the axes split by stage and by how each company sells.
## 3. New entrant gap check
Starts from: competitor-map
Fills: `data.open_space`, `data.candidates`, `data.intro`
Why it gets a reply: it names a gap the account's own team may already be eyeing.
Aim it at accounts that just launched a product and lead with the open space, with the competitor lines below it.
