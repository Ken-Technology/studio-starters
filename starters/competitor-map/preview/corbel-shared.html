The open space

Mid-size contractors who want credit and estimating from one yard

Summit bundles estimating for custom builders and Granger serves production builders on contracts. Nobody serves the contractors between those two with credit, estimating and delivery from one branch network. Corbel already has the credit program and fourteen branches, so estimating is the missing piece.
