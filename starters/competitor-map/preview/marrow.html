The open space

Mid-size sponsors who want the whole suite

Trialdeck sells its suite to everyone and Cohort sells its suite to global pharma only. Nobody sells a full suite built for sponsors with 50 to 200 people, which is where Marrow already has its dashboard customers. Marrow already holds the site data layer those sponsors would start from.
