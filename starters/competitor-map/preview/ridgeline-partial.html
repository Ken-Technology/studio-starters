The open space

Hospitals that want one contract for the whole plant room

Keystone sells projects and services the systems it installs, and Blue Oak sells whole-building contracts to offices. Nobody offers hospitals and labs one maintenance contract that covers HVAC, boilers and chillers together. Ridgeline already holds the HVAC side of those campuses, so the rest of the plant room is the natural next contract.
