The open space

Agencies that pay staff and freelancers from one place

Crewpay handles freelancers and Paymint handles staff, and nobody does both for an agency that runs a mix. Quillpay shows that a product built for one trade can hold its own against the generalists. Tessellate already pays both kinds of worker for agencies and could claim the space by saying so on every page.
