# Ideas
## 1. Speaker session invite
Starts from: conference-meetup
Fills: `data.talk`, `data.personal_line`, `data.why_now`
Why it gets a reply: a seat at a talk is a smaller ask than a meeting.
Lead with your session and have the personal line tie the talk to their news, then offer the meeting after it.
## 2. Side event first
Starts from: conference-meetup
Fills: `data.side_events`, `data.personal_line`, `data.dates_text`
Why it gets a reply: a dinner or breakfast fits a packed show better than a booth visit.
Move the side events up, mark the one with spare seats and make the personal line about that table.
## 3. Two day show
Starts from: conference-meetup
Fills: `data.booth_hours`, `data.booth_note`, `data.dates_text`
Why it gets a reply: fewer days and fixed hours make it easy to pick a slot.
Cut the hours to the one or two days you staff the stand and say who is there each day.
