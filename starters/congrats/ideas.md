# Ideas
## 1. New role welcome
Starts from: congrats
Fills: `data.occasion`, `data.idea_title`, `data.idea`
Why it gets a reply: a new leader is deciding what to fix first and wants outside ideas.
Pick only role and promotion news and make the 90 day idea a plan for the new leader's first quarter.
## 2. Funding round next step
Starts from: congrats
Fills: `data.occasion`, `data.note`, `data.ps`
Why it gets a reply: a fresh round forces choices on where to put the money.
Restrict the occasion to funding and use the note for what a round usually changes for a company their size.
## 3. Launch day follow-up
Starts from: congrats
Fills: `data.occasion`, `data.headline`, `data.ask`
Why it gets a reply: it lands in the days after a launch, when the team is looking for what comes next.
Pick launch news only and send within a week of it, with an ask about what to do in the next 30 days.
