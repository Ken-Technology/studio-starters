Congratulations

Congratulations on the fourth practice, Rhian

Copperfield Dental Group opened its fourth practice, in Sheffield, in October.

Rhian,

Four practices on one booking line and one record system is the point where a dental group stops being a few surgeries that share a phone number. Sheffield is the first one you have opened with the central setup already in place, rather than bolted on afterwards.

That makes it the cleanest test you will get of whether the shared booking line actually fills a new chair.

One idea for the next 90 days

Track how long a new Sheffield patient waits from call to first appointment

For the next 90 days, log every new patient call that the central line routes to Sheffield and the date of the first appointment it turns into. One spreadsheet, two dates per row. By January you will know the real wait at a practice with no backlog, which is the number to hold the next acquisition to.

The groups we work with that measure this at a new site also find out quickly whether the central line sends patients to the nearest practice or to the one with the most free chairs, and those are not always the same thing.

Talk it through

If it would help to talk through how to set the log up so it takes nobody more than a minute a day, I have 15 minutes whenever suits you.
