One idea for the next 90 days

Ask ten existing customers to seed their library before you sell it to anyone new

Clause Library is only useful once it has clauses in it, and the fastest way to fill it is customers who already trust the review product. Pick ten of them, offer to load their twenty most-used clauses by hand, and watch which ones get reused in the first month. Note what they rename and what they leave out.

By January you will know which clause types a buyer expects on day one, which makes the sales conversation for the second product much shorter. The product teams we work with that skip this step usually spend the first quarter guessing.
