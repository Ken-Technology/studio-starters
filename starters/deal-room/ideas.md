# Ideas
## 1. Pilot scoping room
Starts from: deal-room
Fills: `data.facts`, `data.plan`, `data.agenda`
Why it gets a reply: a pilot with named dates and owners is easy to correct and confirm.
Frame the room around one pilot with its scope, a short plan and the first working session as the agenda.
## 2. Security review room
Starts from: deal-room
Fills: `data.resources`, `data.their_people`, `data.plan`
Why it gets a reply: the security reviewer sees every document and open question in one place.
Lead with the security pack and its status, add the reviewer to the people list and plan the review steps.
## 3. Renewal room
Starts from: deal-room
Fills: `data.facts`, `data.note`, `data.lede`
Why it gets a reply: the buyer sees the decision date and what is left before it.
Point the room at an expiring contract, with the renewal date as a key fact and the note on what changed since signing.
