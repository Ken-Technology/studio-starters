# Ideas
## 1. Peer webinar
Starts from: event-invite
Fills: `data.format`, `data.platform`, `data.agenda`
Why it gets a reply: an online session takes less of their evening than a dinner.
Set the format to webinar so the venue and dietary field go away and the form asks for a job title, then write the agenda as topics.
## 2. Waitlist for a full table
Starts from: event-invite
Fills: `data.seats_left`, `data.guests`, `data.why_you`
Why it gets a reply: a full table makes the seat feel worth asking for.
Set the seats to zero so the stamp reads full and the form becomes a waitlist, and keep the note about why they belong.
## 3. Roles-only guest list
Starts from: event-invite
Fills: `data.guests`, `data.why_you`, `data.why_now`
Why it gets a reply: a reader sees at once whether the room is made of their peers.
Narrow the guest line to one role, such as finance leaders only, and tie the personal line to that role.
