Three things for Brightwave Logistics

Picked for your role as VP Operations at Brightwave Logistics.

  1. 1

    Run the audit on dispatch and billing first

    The median across 40 teams was 31 hours per 100 loads. Teams that had added sites sat at 36 to 40, and Brightwave opened two cross-dock sites this year.

    Chapter at 2:40
  2. 2

    Centralize exceptions before anything else

    Kestrel moved exception handling to one desk before touching dispatch. It gave back the most hours per month of any change, which matters most when volume is growing.

    Chapter at 26:05
  3. 3

    A new site does not need a planner on day one

    Priya's answer on new cross-docks was to run the site from the central desk for a quarter, then decide. With two sites opened this year, that is a live question for operations.
