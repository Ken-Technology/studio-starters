Three things for Ridgeview Dental Group

Picked for your role as Director of Patient Operations at Ridgeview Dental Group.

  1. 1

    Ask patients to confirm or cancel

    A confirm or cancel reply cut no-shows more than a plain reminder did. Cancelled slots could be rebooked, which helps a group that runs many schedules.

    Chapter at 8:45
  2. 2

    Keep a same-day waitlist

    Practices with a same-day waitlist refilled about a third of cancelled slots. With two new offices this year, empty chairs cost more to leave open.

    Chapter at 15:20
  3. 3

    Check how it works across several offices

    The questions at the end cover scheduling across several offices and insurance checks. Both come up for a patient operations team running one shared desk.

    Chapter at 33:40
