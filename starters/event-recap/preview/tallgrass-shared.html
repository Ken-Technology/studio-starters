Three things for Tallgrass Packaging

Picked for Tallgrass Packaging.

  1. 1

    Count the waiting, not only the work

    Cleaning, setup and checks took most of the time, but waiting for tools or approvals took more than people expected. A log shows it per line.

    Chapter at 4:30
  2. 2

    Stage a tool kit at each line

    One plant took its busiest line from 25 minutes to under 10 by staging the kit before the last job ended. Tallgrass has a sixth line to set up this way.

    Chapter at 12:10
  3. 3

    Let the operator sign off the first article

    Moving first-article sign-off to the operator, with a photo standard, removed the longest wait in the study.
