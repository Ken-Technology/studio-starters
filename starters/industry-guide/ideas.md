# Ideas
## 1. Role playbook for one title
Starts from: industry-guide
Fills: `data.role`, `data.moves`, `data.title`
Why it gets a reply: the moves are written for their job.
Send one version per title in the account, such as finance or operations, with the five moves written from that seat.
## 2. Playbook for a new trigger
Starts from: industry-guide
Fills: `data.signals`, `data.dossier`, `data.closing`
Why it gets a reply: it ties the moves to news they just had.
Aim it at accounts with fresh news such as a new site or a new hire, and let the closing say which move fits that news.
## 3. Your point of view as moves
Starts from: industry-guide
Fills: `data.offer`, `data.pov`, `data.moves`
Why it gets a reply: the guide reads as a way of working they can try without you.
Load your method into Knowledge so the five moves follow it, then keep the first step of each move free to do this week.
