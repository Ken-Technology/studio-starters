# Ideas
## 1. Hiring surge report
Starts from: market-report
Fills: `data.signal_defs`, `data.hiring_defs`, `data.plays`
Why it gets a reply: it names how many peers are hiring right now.
Point the filters at hiring surges and funding, put the growth signals first and make the three plays about reaching those companies.
## 2. Regional market report
Starts from: market-report
Fills: `data.region_defs`, `data.scope`, `data.narrative`
Why it gets a reply: the count is for the account's own region, not a global figure.
Narrow the market to one country or state and break the regions section into its cities or sub-regions.
## 3. Buyer map for a new product
Starts from: market-report
Fills: `data.role_small_defs`, `data.role_large_defs`, `data.brief`
Why it gets a reply: it sizes the exact job titles the account would have to reach.
Lead with the buyers section, count the roles your product sells to and let the brief open on the people counts.
