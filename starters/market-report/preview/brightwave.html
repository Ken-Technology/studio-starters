2Who is in the market

The profile is a US company with 200 to 1,000 staff whose main business is moving or storing freight. There are 4,170 of them. Trucking carriers and brokers are the two largest segments, which is where Brightwave's direct competitors sit. Warehousing and forwarding come next, and they buy differently.

3Where they are

Freight follows the interstates and the ports. The Midwest holds 1,293 of the 4,170 companies, more than any other region, and the South is close behind with 1,126.

For Brightwave this cuts both ways. The Midwest has the most companies bidding on the same shippers, and it is the region the size comparison in section 6 is drawn from.

4Who buys

Five titles decide on operations spend in this market. Across the 4,170 companies, 9,270 people hold one of them. At the smaller companies there are more people in every role than at the larger ones.

VP or Director of Operations is the largest group at 2,960 people, and Director of Logistics comes next at 2,310. Together they are the best place to start.

5Who is growing

Two signals say a company may be about to change how it runs operations: it is hiring for operations, or it has new money. Both come from job posts and funding announcements.

  • 1,107Hiring in operations. 27% of the market.
  • 86Raised in 12 months. 2% of the market.
  • 61Hiring and raised. 1% of the market.

Brokers and 3PLs have 323 companies hiring, the most of any segment. 86 companies raised money in the last twelve months, and 61 of them are also hiring.

6Where Brightwave Logistics sits

Brightwave is in the 201 to 500 band. The peer group is every freight and logistics company in its region, split by headcount, with the share of each band that is hiring.

7Three plays

For Brightwave Logistics, drawn from the counts in this report.

  • Start with the 1,107 companies that are hiring

    A company hiring for operations is changing how it runs them. 1,107 companies in the market are hiring right now, and 323 of them are brokers and 3PLs. Write to the operations lead about what the new hires will inherit.

  • Sell to operations, not procurement

    There are 2,960 VP and Director of Operations titles across the market, 1,840 of them at companies with 200 to 500 staff. Write to the operator about lanes, detention and ETAs, not about rates.

  • Reach the funded companies early

    86 companies raised money in the last twelve months and 61 of them are hiring. New money tends to turn into new hires and new vendors within a few quarters, so a first message now arrives before their plans are set.
