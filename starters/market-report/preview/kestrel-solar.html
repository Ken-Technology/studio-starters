Who is in the market

The profile is a US company with 10 to 200 staff that installs solar for homes or small businesses. Ken Search finds 3,300 of them. Residential installers are the biggest group, and that is where Kestrel works.

Commercial, industrial is the second largest segment at 720 companies. Roofing and solar is the smallest at 330.

3

Where they are

The South holds 1,260 of the 3,300 companies and the West holds 1,190, so two regions cover most of the market. The Midwest has the fewest at 310.

Kestrel sells in the West, the second largest region, where 1,190 installers compete for the same homeowners.

4

Who is growing

A company that is hiring salespeople or has new money is about to spend on how it finds homeowners. Ken Search tracks hiring from job posts and funding from announcements.

612Hiring in sales. 19% of the market.
74Raised in 12 months. 2% of the market.
39Hiring and raised. 1% of the market.

Residential installers have 420 companies hiring, the most of any segment. 74 companies raised money in the last twelve months, and 39 of them are also hiring.

5

Three plays for Kestrel Solar

  1. Write to the 612 companies hiring in sales

    A company hiring salespeople is about to need more leads for them. 612 companies are hiring now, and 420 of them are residential installers. Write to the sales lead about what the new reps will work from.

  2. Focus on residential installers first

    Residential installers are 1,840 of the 3,300 companies and the largest hiring group at 420. The pitch is the same for most of them, so one message can be tuned for the whole segment.

  3. Reach the 39 that are funded and hiring

    74 companies raised money in the last twelve months and 39 of them are hiring. A funded installer tends to add crews and vendors fast, so a message now arrives early.
