2Who is in the market

The profile is a US company with 10 to 200 staff that installs solar for homes or small businesses. There are 3,300 of them. Residential installers are the biggest group, and that is where Kestrel works.

3Where they are

The South holds 1,260 of the 3,300 companies and the West holds 1,190, so two regions cover most of the market. The Midwest has the fewest at 310.

Kestrel sells in the West, the second largest region, where 1,190 installers compete for the same homeowners.

4Who is growing

A company that is hiring salespeople or has new money is about to spend on how it finds homeowners. Hiring comes from job posts and funding from announcements.

5Three plays

For Kestrel Solar, drawn from the counts in this report.

  • Write to the 612 companies hiring in sales

    A company hiring salespeople is about to need more leads for them. 612 companies are hiring now, and 420 of them are residential installers. Write to the sales lead about what the new reps will work from.

  • Focus on residential installers first

    Residential installers are 1,840 of the 3,300 companies and the largest hiring group at 420. The pitch is the same for most of them, so one message can be tuned for the whole segment.

  • Reach the 39 that are funded and hiring

    74 companies raised money in the last twelve months and 39 of them are hiring. A funded installer tends to add crews and vendors fast, so a message now arrives early.
