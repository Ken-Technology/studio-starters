2

Who is in the market

The profile is a US software company with 50 to 500 staff that sells into HR, payroll or hiring teams. There are 2,710 of them. HR management is the largest segment, and payroll, where Lumen sits, is the second largest.

Time and attendance is the smallest segment at 260 companies. Recruiting software has 520, more than benefits administration at 380.

3

Where they are

The West holds 760 of the 2,710 companies, the most of any region. The South follows with 690 and the Northeast with 610.

Lumen is based in the South, where 690 companies compete for the same small employers. The Mountain region is the smallest with 200.

4

Who buys

Four titles decide on sales and marketing spend in this market. Across the 2,710 companies, 6,760 people hold one of them. There are more of them at companies with 50 to 200 staff in every role but one.

Chief Revenue Officer is the exception, with 380 people at the larger companies and 310 at the smaller ones. At 50 to 200 staff the VP of Sales is the biggest group, with 1,980 people.

5

Who is growing

A company that is hiring salespeople or has new money is about to spend on its revenue stack. Hiring comes from job posts and funding from announcements.

780Hiring in sales. 29% of the market.
142Raised in 12 months. 5% of the market.
96Hiring and raised. 4% of the market.

HR management software has 290 companies hiring, the most of any segment. 142 companies raised money in the last twelve months, and 96 of them are also hiring.

6

Where Lumen Payroll sits

Lumen is in the 51 to 200 band. The peer group is every HR and payroll software company in the South, split by headcount, with the number in each band that are hiring.

Lumen's band is the largest in the South with 261 companies, and 88 of them are hiring. The next band up, 201 to 500, has 118 companies and 42 of them are hiring.

7

Three plays for Lumen Payroll

  1. Write to the 780 companies hiring in sales

    A company hiring salespeople is about to need a way to reach new buyers. 780 companies are hiring now, and 290 of them sell HR management software. Write to the head of sales about ramping new reps.

  2. Go to the VP of Sales at smaller companies

    There are 1,980 VPs of Sales at companies with 50 to 200 staff, the largest single group in the report. They run the team and own the number, so write about pipeline, not about tools.

  3. Reach the 96 that are funded and hiring

    142 companies raised money in the last twelve months and 96 of them are hiring. A funded company tends to pick vendors in the first few quarters, so a message now lands early.
