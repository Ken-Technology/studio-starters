2

Who is in the market

The market here is every UK company in dental and oral care, from single practices that have grown into groups to labs and specialist clinics. Ken Search finds 2,850. Dental practice groups are the biggest segment, which is where Meridian sits.

Orthodontics is the second largest segment at 610 companies. Multi-site clinics are the smallest at 150, but 45 of them are hiring.

3

Where they are

London holds 640 of the 2,850 companies, the most of any region. The South East has 590 and the Midlands 560, so the market is spread across the south and centre of the country.

Meridian is based in London, the largest region, so it has the most peers close by. Scotland and Wales is the smallest, with 270 companies.

4

Who buys

Four titles run the business side of a dental group. Across the 2,850 companies, 5,650 people hold one of them. Most practice managers work at the smaller companies.

Practice manager is the largest group by a wide margin. Operations director and finance director are the two roles with more people at the larger companies.

5

Who is growing

A group that is hiring or has new money is a group that is changing how its clinics run. Ken Search tracks hiring from job posts and funding from announcements.

Dental practice groups have 310 companies hiring, the most of any segment. 38 companies raised money in the last twelve months, and 27 of them are also hiring.

Where Meridian Dental Partners sits

Meridian is in the 51 to 200 band. The peer group is every dental and oral care company in London, split by headcount, with the number in each band that are hiring.

Meridian's band holds 188 London companies and 51 of them are hiring. The smallest band is the biggest, with 311 companies, and 68 of those are hiring.

7

Three plays for Meridian Dental Partners

  1. Talk to the 540 groups that are hiring

    A group hiring for operations is rebuilding how its clinics are run. 540 companies are hiring now, and 310 of them are dental practice groups. Write to the operations lead about the first month of a new hire.

  2. Reach practice managers first

    There are 2,950 practice managers in the market, 2,310 of them at companies with 11 to 200 staff. They run the day to day and feel the pain first, so write about their week, not about the group.

  3. Look at the 27 that are funded and hiring

    38 companies raised money in the last twelve months and 27 of them are hiring. New money often means new clinics, so a message now lands before their systems are chosen.
