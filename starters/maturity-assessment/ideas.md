# Ideas
## 1. Security readiness check
Starts from: maturity-assessment
Fills: `data.questions`, `data.dimensions`, `data.steps`
Why it gets a reply: a low score in one area gives the sender a specific thing to discuss.
Swap the eight questions and four areas for security basics and keep the scoring, the radar and the three next steps.
## 2. Peer benchmark first
Starts from: maturity-assessment
Fills: `data.peers`, `data.intro`, `account.industry`
Why it gets a reply: people want to see where they stand against companies like theirs.
Open the start view on the peer group and its assessment count, and lead the result with the peer comparison.
## 3. Pre-call scorecard
Starts from: maturity-assessment
Fills: `data.headline`, `data.stages`, `person.first_name`
Why it gets a reply: it turns a booked call into a reviewed score.
Send it after a demo request, rename the stages to your own maturity ladder and ask them to fill it in before the call.
