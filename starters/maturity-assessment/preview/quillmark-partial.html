Three moves that usually come next

  1. Write down the ten decisions your team makes mostProcess, of 4

    Ten decisions, one page each. New hires ramp faster, and you see which steps a system could take over.

  2. Agree on five Monday numbers and one view that holds themData, of 4

    Choose the five figures every manager reads on Monday, build the single view that shows them, and retire the spreadsheets that fed the old ones.

  3. Put the three most repeated handoffs on rulesAutomation, of 4

    Pick the three handoffs your team repeats most each day and let software do them before you add people. It is usually where the first month of time comes back.

  4. Name one owner for improvement, with two hours a weekTeam, of 4

    One person, a backlog, and a slot every week. It is the cheapest change on this page and it makes the other moves stick.
