Three moves that usually come next

  1. Write down the steps of one job from quote to final walkthroughProcess, of 4

    One page for the job you do most often. The next crew lead starts from it instead of from memory, and you see which steps slip.

  2. Keep job costs in one place and read them every FridayData, of 4

    Put estimates, change orders and actual costs in one sheet or tool. Review it weekly so a job that runs over shows up while there is still time to act.

  3. Put change orders and scheduling reminders on rulesAutomation, of 4

    Pick the two messages your office sends most each week and let software send them. It gives the office back time without adding a person.

  4. Name one person to own how jobs get runTeam, of 4

    One owner, a short list, and an hour a week. In a firm of thirty, the owner usually holds this role without calling it that.
