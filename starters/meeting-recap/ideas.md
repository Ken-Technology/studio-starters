# Ideas
## 1. Demo follow-up
Starts from: meeting-recap
Fills: `data.heard`, `data.agreed`, `data.resources`
Why it gets a reply: it sends the buyer a page they can forward to the people who missed the demo.
Use a product demo as the call, list the questions the buyer asked under what we heard and put the demo links under what we mentioned.
## 2. Mutual action plan
Starts from: meeting-recap
Fills: `data.steps`, `data.call`, `sender.name`
Why it gets a reply: every task has an owner and a date, so replying with a change is easy.
Lead with the next steps table, give each side the same number of tasks and set a date the next call has to happen by.
## 3. Discovery call summary
Starts from: meeting-recap
Fills: `data.note`, `data.heard`, `person.first_name`
Why it gets a reply: it shows the account that the sender listened.
Write it after a first call, keep what we heard long and the agreed list short and ask them to correct anything that is off.
