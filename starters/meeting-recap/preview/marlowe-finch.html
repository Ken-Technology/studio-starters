What we heard

The firm
You are 62 people across Portland, Salem and Bend, with about 1,400 business tax clients and 900 individual returns. Bend is the smallest office at 9 staff.
Tax season documents
Clients send PDFs and phone photos to whichever staff member they know, so documents land in personal inboxes and get forwarded around. Your staff chase what is missing by phone and email.
Extensions
Extensions were up 18 percent in the 2025 season compared with 2024. Priya thinks late documents are most of that.
Current tools
A document vault for storage and email for everything else. There is no client portal today, and signing happens in a separate tool that confuses clients.
What you want
One link per client to upload to, reminders that go out on their own, and a list where staff see who has sent what. Ingrid wants engagement letters and Form 8879 signed in the same place.
Timing
Sam would rather start with the Bend office than move all 2,300 client folders at once, and wants it running before January 15. The partners meet on November 12 and need a proposal before then.

What we agreed

  1. A 60 day pilot in the Bend office, starting Tuesday, December 1.

  2. Bend goes first. The other offices' client folders stay in the vault for now.

  3. The partners see a proposal before their meeting on November 12.

  4. We walk through the pilot plan together on Tuesday, November 10, and Ana shows the client reminder flow on that call.
