What we heard

Your situation, as you described it on the call.

Claims volume
About 38,000 auto and home claims a year. Around 70 percent come in by phone and the rest through a web form that writes into a claims system from 2009.
Phone intake
A phone intake takes 22 minutes on average, which Dana measured in September. Much of that is the adjuster typing while the caller looks for the policy number.
Photos and status
Adjusters re-key the photos policyholders email in. Policyholders call back to ask for status because there is no status page or text.
What Robert wants
Photo upload at first notice, status texts and a shorter phone intake. The core system stays this year.
The nightly batch
The core system has no live API and takes a batch file once a night. Anything we build has to feed that batch, and Luis wants a technical review before he sees a proposal.

What we agreed

The decisions both sides made on the call.

  1. Scope is auto claims only. Home claims have a different intake and wait.

  2. The core claims system stays. We work with the nightly batch, not around it.

  3. Luis's team gets a technical review before Pinecrest sees a proposal.

  4. Robert takes the proposal to the Q4 operations review on December 3.
