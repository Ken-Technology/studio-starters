What we heard

The clinics
Six clinics, 14 providers and about 1,100 visits a week.
No-shows
The no-show rate was 11 percent in September. Marcus pulled the report last week.
Reminder calls
On Thursday afternoons two front desk staff call next week's list by hand. It takes most of the afternoon and people still get missed.
Recall letters
Well-visit recall letters go out by mail twice a year.
Texting
Parents ask for text reminders and the clinics have none today. Your EHR stays with its current vendor.
Clinical review
Dr. Rahman's team reads every parent text before it goes out, and a text never says why the child is coming in.

What we agreed

  1. A 30 day trial at the Lakeview and Northgate clinics, starting Tuesday, November 17.

  2. Texts say the time, the clinic and how to reschedule. Never a diagnosis or a reason for the visit.

  3. The clinical team reviews every text before the trial goes live.

  4. After the trial, Marcus gets a number for the other four clinics before the year ends.
