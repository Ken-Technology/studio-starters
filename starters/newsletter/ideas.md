# Ideas
## 1. Quarterly industry letter
Starts from: newsletter
Fills: `data.note`, `data.headline`, `account.industry`
Why it gets a reply: a slower rhythm reads as thoughtful, not as a drip campaign.
Change the cadence to every quarter, let the industry note carry most of the page and shorten the idea.
## 2. Customer story as the resource
Starts from: newsletter
Fills: `data.resource`, `data.idea`, `data.ps`
Why it gets a reply: a story from a company like theirs is easy to forward.
Fill the resource from your customer stories in Knowledge and let the idea borrow one move from that story.
## 3. Post-meeting brief
Starts from: newsletter
Fills: `data.intro`, `person.first_name`, `data.idea`
Why it gets a reply: it follows a conversation they already had with you.
Send it a week after a first call, open the intro with what the call was about and make the idea a next step they can take alone.
