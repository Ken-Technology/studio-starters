In your industry

An imaging center earns its reputation in the first month

Referring physicians decide quickly whether a new imaging site is easy to send patients to. What they judge is not the equipment. It is how long their patient waited for a slot and whether the report came back on time.

Your site lists central scheduling across all Ashgrove locations, so the people booking the new Ohio centers already handle the rest of the network. That is an advantage, as long as the new sites get the same attention as the busy ones in the first weeks.

The usual pattern is the opposite: the new site has open slots, the older sites have queues, and nobody moves patients between them.

What prompted it

Two imaging centers opened in Ohio this quarter, and your site routes all booking through central scheduling

02

One idea for Ashgrove Health Clinics

Run a two-week no-show review for the new Ohio sites

A new site with open capacity hides its no-shows, because an empty slot does not look like a loss yet. By the time the schedule is full, the pattern is set.

For the first two weeks, have one scheduler pull every missed imaging appointment at the two new centers each morning and note the reason given, if any. Group them by referring practice and by time of day.

The list usually points at a few referring practices and one time block. That is a phone call, not a project.

Where to start

Ask one scheduler to pull yesterday's missed imaging appointments at the two Ohio centers tomorrow morning and write down the reasons.
