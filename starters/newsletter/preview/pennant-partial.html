01

In your industry

Group business is the one revenue line each hotel still owns

Transient rates at a franchised hotel are mostly set by the brand's systems and the market. Group and events revenue is different: it is sold by a person at the property, on the phone, from a lead that came in that week.

Your site lists group sales and events contacts on every property page, which means the quality of that revenue line depends on how each of those people spends a Tuesday afternoon.

That is not a weakness. It is the part of the business where a small change in habit shows up in the numbers within a quarter.

02

One idea for Pennant Hospitality Group

Ask every sales manager for last month's lost group leads

Won business gets reported. Lost leads mostly get deleted. Yet the lost list is where the pattern is: the same competitor hotel, the same date block, the same missing meeting room.

Ask each property's sales manager for a short list of the group leads they lost last month, with the reason they believe it went elsewhere. Do not build a form for it. A reply to an email is fine.

Read all of them in one sitting. You will know more about your group pricing after an hour than any report shows.

Where to start

Send one email to each property's sales manager today asking for last month's lost group leads and the reason for each.
