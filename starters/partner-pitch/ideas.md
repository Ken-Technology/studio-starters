# Ideas
## 1. Referral partner pitch
Starts from: partner-pitch
Fills: `data.offer`, `data.first_step`, `data.our_offer`
Why it gets a reply: a referral swap costs the partner almost nothing to try.
Make the joint offer a two-way referral, with each side sending the other its best-fit customers, and shrink the first step to one shared list.
## 2. Co-hosted webinar invite
Starts from: partner-pitch
Fills: `data.offer`, `data.examples`, `data.why_now`
Why it gets a reply: it is one hour of work and a shared audience.
Turn the joint offer into a webinar for the companies in the overlap, and let the example companies show who would be invited.
## 3. Integration partner pitch
Starts from: partner-pitch
Fills: `data.partner_offer`, `data.overlap`, `data.headline`
Why it gets a reply: it ties the overlap to something their customers already ask for.
Frame the overlap as shared customers who run both tools, and make the offer a listed integration with a joint announcement.
