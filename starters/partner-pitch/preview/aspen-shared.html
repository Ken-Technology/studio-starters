The joint offer

Equipment hours that set the replacement plan

Contractors on Fieldstone get a leasing offer from Aspen built on the hours their machines ran last season, instead of a guess. You get a lessee who knows which five machines to replace. We get a referral partner that sees contractors the week they lease a new fleet.

Aspen Equipment Finance brings

  • Seasonal leases and loans for mowers, loaders, trucks and snow equipment, decided in two business days
  • A dedicated contact for referral partners
  • Contractors replacing part of a fleet every spring

Fieldstone brings

  • Hours per machine from crew clock-ins, which a contractor can share with you when they ask for a lease
  • Routes and time tracking the crews already use, so the hours are there without extra work
  • Around 900 contractors, most of them in your states

The customer gets

A replacement plan and a lease built on the hours each machine actually ran, from two companies that already know their crews and their fleet.

How it runs

  1. Agree what a contractor sharesWe settle on one export: machine, hours last season, crew. The contractor chooses to send it to Aspen. Nothing moves without them.
  2. Add Fieldstone to your landscaping pageYour new landscaping page lists us as a way to track machine hours. Our help center lists Aspen as a way to finance a replacement.
  3. Refer both ways through one contactYour partner contact and I trade introductions every two weeks and keep one shared list of who was referred.
  4. Check the first seasonAfter the spring season we look at how many contractors used the export and whether their lease decisions changed.

The first step

A 30 minute call on the machine hours export

I show you the equipment hours a contractor sees in Fieldstone, and you tell me whether that is what your underwriters want to see for a replacement lease. If it is, we write the export together. If it is not, we know what to change.
