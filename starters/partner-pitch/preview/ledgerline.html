Your side comes from your website. Ours comes from what we know about our own customers.

ToYou sell to independent restaurants, cafes and bars, and to restaurant groups with up to 50 locations across the US. Your system runs the front of house and the timeclock, and groups use it to see sales, labor and tips per location in one dashboard.

Where they meet: Restaurant groups with 3 to 30 locations

  • The same buyer: the owner or controller who signs for the POS also signs for payroll.
  • The same moment: a third or fourth site is when one menu, one dashboard and one payroll stop being optional.
  • The same hours: staff clock in on your terminal, and those hours are what we run payroll from.
  • The same map: your groups are across the US, and most of ours are in Texas, the Southeast and the Midwest.

Tallgrass

SellsPayroll and HR software for restaurant groups: payroll from timeclock hours, tip pooling and tip credits by state, and payroll tax filings.

ToRestaurant groups with 3 to 30 locations and 40 to 600 hourly staff, mostly in Texas, the Southeast and the Midwest. The buyer is the owner, the controller or the director of operations. Most come to us when they open a third or fourth site and tips, overtime and state filings stop fitting in a spreadsheet.

Payroll that reads the Ledgerline timeclock

A bundle for restaurant groups that run on Ledgerline: timeclock hours flow into Tallgrass payroll at the end of each pay period, with tips and overtime already coded per location. You offer it to groups that are adding sites. We offer Ledgerline to groups that come to us without a timeclock worth keeping.

The customer gets

One timeclock, one payroll run and one set of tip rules per location, set up in a week instead of a month, with nobody keying hours twice.

Ledgerline POS brings

  • A timeclock export per location, with breaks, overtime and tips, that your groups already use
  • A partner program and a partnerships hire to run joint deals through
  • Groups that are adding sites, which is the week they go looking for payroll

Tallgrass brings

  • Payroll from your hours export, with tip pooling and tip credits by state
  • Payroll tax filings in every state your groups operate in
  • An onboarding team that already maps POS exports by hand, and would map yours once

How it runs

  1. Map the export onceOur onboarding team maps your timeclock export to our payroll fields one time, so every shared customer gets the same setup.
  2. Offer it to groups adding a siteWhen a group on Ledgerline opens a third or fourth location, your team mentions the bundle. We do the same for groups that come to us first.
  3. Onboard in a weekHours flow from your terminals, we run the first payroll next to their old process, and the group switches when the numbers match.
  4. Review every quarterWe compare who signed, who stayed and what broke, and decide together whether to widen it.

A 30 minute call to compare ten customers

You bring ten groups on Ledgerline that are adding sites this year. I bring ten of ours that run on a POS with a timeclock. If five of them look like the same company, we have an offer. If not, we both learned something in half an hour.
