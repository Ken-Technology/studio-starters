The joint offer

A compliance path for every Northgate customer

Software companies on Northgate get SOC 2 or ISO 27001 readiness with the hosting controls already covered by your matrix and your certified data centers. You offer it when a customer asks for a SOC 2 report at renewal. We offer Northgate to customers whose hosting cannot pass an audit.

Northgate Cloud brings

  • ISO 27001 certified data centers and a shared responsibility matrix our customers can cite as evidence
  • An alliances program and the renewal conversation where a customer first asks about SOC 2
  • More than 400 software companies on your platform, most of them selling to enterprises

Kestrel Compliance brings

  • Evidence collection from the cloud account, the identity provider and the ticketing tool, with no agent on your clusters
  • A named compliance lead who runs the audit with the customer
  • A hosting control set mapped to your matrix once, so every shared customer starts with it filled in

The customer gets

An audit where the hosting controls are answered on day one, by the two companies that run the cluster and collect the evidence, instead of by the customer's own engineers.

How it runs

  1. Map the matrix to our controlsWe map your shared responsibility matrix to the SOC 2 and ISO 27001 hosting controls once. Every shared customer inherits it.
  2. Add a line to your renewal checklistWhen a customer asks for a SOC 2 report or an ISO certificate, your account manager introduces us. We do the same for hosting.
  3. Run the first three audits togetherWe take three shared customers through the audit and note what the auditor still asked about hosting.
  4. Decide on a bundleAfter the three audits we decide whether to sell it as one package or keep it as a referral.

The first step

A 30 minute call with your alliances lead

We walk through your shared responsibility matrix next to our hosting control list and count how many controls it already answers. You leave with that count. I leave knowing whether the mapping is a week of work or a month.

What I need from youSend the current matrix before the call, and tell me who owns renewals on your side.
