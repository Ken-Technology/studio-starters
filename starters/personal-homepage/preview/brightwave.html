How a rollout would go at Brightwave Logistics

Ten weeks from the first connection to all seven terminals, with Joliet as the pilot site because that is where the new volume and the open roles are.

  1. Weeks 1 and 2

    Connect

    We connect your TMS and the Joliet dispatch board. Your security review runs alongside. Nothing changes for dispatchers yet.

  2. Weeks 3 to 6

    Pilot at Joliet

    Joliet's dispatchers run the exception board for 30 days with a weekly check-in. We compare late-load calls against September.

  3. Weeks 7 to 10

    All seven terminals

    Two terminals a week, with the Joliet team training the next site. You get the capacity view across all lanes at the end.

Questions people ask before a first call

Does this replace our TMS?

No. It reads from it. Dispatchers keep the TMS for planning and use Northbeam for exceptions and handoffs. Nothing is written back unless you ask for it.

How long does the first connection take?

The first two weeks of a rollout are for connecting the TMS and one dispatch board. Dispatchers change nothing in that time.

What does the pilot cost?

We share pricing on the call, so you see the numbers next to the pilot plan.

How much time does it take from the dispatch team?

That depends on the setup, and we go through it on the call. The pilot site's dispatchers are the ones who use the board day to day.
