The rollout

Start with one clinic, then add the other four, so the front desks learn it on one schedule before it reaches all five.

  1. Weeks 1 and 2

    Connect one clinic

    We read the schedule from the clinic's practice management system and set up its vets, rooms and visit types.

  2. Weeks 3 to 4

    Open online booking

    Owners start booking and confirming online. The front desk keeps the phone and sees every change on the day sheet.

  3. Weeks 5 to 10

    Add the other clinics

    One clinic every two weeks, with the first clinic's front desk showing the next one how it works.

Questions

What people ask before a first call with Jonas.

Do we have to change our practice management system?

No. Halcyon reads the schedule from it, and clinics keep using it for records and billing.

What happens when an owner cannot make a visit?

They tap reschedule in the reminder, pick another open time, and the clinic's day sheet updates.

How long does setup take for each clinic?

About two weeks per clinic. We start with one and add the others after.

What does it cost?

We share pricing on the call, so you see it next to the plan for five clinics.
