How a rollout would go at Marlow Packaging

Start with one die-cutting line, then add the second once the schedule has run through a few weeks of production.

  1. Weeks 1 and 2

    Set up line one

    We load the line's machines and their service history, and agree the first month of planned work with your team.

  2. Weeks 3 to 4

    Run it on the floor

    Operators write notes on their phones and the technician works from the schedule. We check in each week.

  3. Weeks 5 and 6

    Add the second line

    The second die-cutting line goes on the same schedule, and you see both lines in one view.

Questions people ask before a first call

Do we have to drop our spreadsheets?

No. Forgeline works alongside them, and you can move over one machine at a time.

Who needs training?

Operators need ten minutes to write a note. The maintenance technician needs a short walkthrough of the schedule.

How long until the first line is live?

About two weeks to set up one line, and the full rollout takes about six weeks.

What does it cost?

We share pricing on the call, so you see it next to the plan for two lines.
