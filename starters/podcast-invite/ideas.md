# Ideas
## 1. Written interview series
Starts from: podcast-invite
Fills: `data.show_format`, `data.episode_length`, `data.recording`
Why it gets a reply: answering in writing at their own pace asks for 20 minutes, not a recording slot.
Set the show format to written, so the page asks them to say yes, answer the questions and approve the edit.
## 2. Customer guest episode
Starts from: podcast-invite
Fills: `data.audience`, `data.recent_episodes`, `data.angle`
Why it gets a reply: a customer gets to talk about their own work to their peers.
Invite your own customers, list past guests as the recent episodes and make the angle the decision they made and why.
## 3. Event live recording
Starts from: podcast-invite
Fills: `data.recording`, `data.published_on`, `data.invite_line`
Why it gets a reply: it fits into a trip they already have booked.
Record at a conference booth or room, put the time and place in the recording line and open the invite on that event.
