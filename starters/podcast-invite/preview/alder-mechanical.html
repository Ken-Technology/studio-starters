The angle

Bringing a 24-hour service desk in-house at Alder Mechanical

We would spend most of the time on the service desk: why you ended the answering service, how you staffed dispatchers around the clock, and what the first month taught you about the night-to-morning handoff and the parts van nobody restocked. Then the third branch: how Spokane shares one desk and one board with Seattle and Portland, and what you would set up differently if you opened a fourth. No product talk from our side and no company history, just the decisions and what they cost.

Three questions we would start with

  1. What made you end eight years with the answering service, and what was the first week like?

    From your post on bringing the service desk in-house.

  2. How do three branches with their own crews share one service desk without fighting over the board?

    Your site says every branch runs its own crews and shares one desk, and the dispatch team wrote about keeping one board.

  3. If you opened a fourth branch next year, what would you set up on day one that Spokane did not have?

    The Spokane branch opened in September.
