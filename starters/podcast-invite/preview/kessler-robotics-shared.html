The angle

Shipping software to 400 robots in other people's warehouses

We would dig into the fleet service: how updates go out in waves during charging windows, what the service watches to decide a wave is safe, and the March rollback, from the first sign that pickers were waiting on robots to the two checks you added afterwards. Then hiring: what you look for in a fleet software engineer now that the team is growing. No founding story and no product talk from our side, just the system and the calls behind it.
