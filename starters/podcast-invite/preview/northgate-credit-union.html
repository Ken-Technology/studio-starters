The angle

Listening to 22 branches before changing the lending book

The piece would follow the branch visits: what the lending teams told you, where small business lending has grown fastest since the program opened in 2023, and which parts of a regional bank's commercial lending playbook carry over to a credit union and which do not. We would end on the lending book itself: how you decide what to grow next, and how you explain that to a board. No product talk from our side and no company history. Your answers run as written, edited only for length.

Three questions we would start with

  1. 1

    What did the lending teams tell you in the branch visits that you did not expect?

    From your note on joining Northgate, which set out to visit all 22 branches by the end of the year.

  2. 2

    Which parts of a regional bank's commercial lending playbook carried over to Northgate, and which have you dropped?

    You spent twelve years in commercial lending at a regional bank before joining.

  3. 3

    How do you explain to the board where the lending book should grow next, and what you are saying no to?

    Northgate's business lending team has written loans for owner-operated businesses since 2023.

You get the full set of questions once you say yes, and you can strike any of them.
