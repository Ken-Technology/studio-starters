1The problem in Redhill Fabrication's words

These are the three things we heard on Oct 15 and Oct 23, as close to how they were said as our notes allow.

We lose bids on speed. By the time our quote lands the buyer has two others and a favorite.

Ruth Castellano, Operations Manager, Oct 15

2What we propose

One quote builder for the sheet-metal line, fed by your drawings and your ERP price list. The platform reads the price list; nothing is written back.

In scope for the pilot

  • Read-only import of the ERP price list for sheet-metal parts
  • Quote draft built from the drawing set, with every price line traceable
  • Review screen for Gary and the second estimator before a quote goes out
  • Quote status board for Pablo's sales team
  • Weekly 30-minute review with Ruth and Kit

Out of scope until year one

  • Structural quotes
  • Write-back to the ERP
  • Customer portal

If the pilot passes, year one covers sheet-metal and structural quoting for the whole plant, up to 8 users.
