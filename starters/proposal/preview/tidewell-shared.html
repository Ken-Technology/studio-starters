1The problem in Tidewell Dental Group's words

These are the three things we heard on Oct 12 and Oct 22, as close to how they were said as our notes allow.

A referral goes out the door and we never see it again. The patient tells us three weeks later that nobody called.

Ines Calder, Chief Operating Officer, Oct 12

My front desk keeps referrals in a shared inbox and a binder. When someone is off, the binder wins.

Rosa Delgado, Regional Clinic Manager, Oct 12

New patients fill in a paper form on arrival. That is ten minutes of the first appointment gone.

Marcus Webb, Director of Patient Access, Oct 22

What it costs today

From Rosa's September referral log for Riverside and Eastgate, shared Oct 20.

61 of 212specialist referrals had no confirmed appointment after 14 days
9 ha week at each front desk spent chasing referrals
38%of new patients completed their forms before arrival

The referrals are sent. They are not tracked after that. Each clinic keeps its own record, so nobody can see which ones are open.

2What we propose

One referral tracker and one digital intake form for the front desk, starting at two clinics. The platform reads from your scheduling system; nothing is written back.

In scope for the pilot

  • Read-only connector to the scheduling system at Riverside and Eastgate
  • Referral tracker with an owner and a next step on every open referral
  • Digital new-patient form, sent before the first visit
  • Single sign-on through Google Workspace
  • Weekly 30-minute review with Rosa and Anika

Out of scope until year one

  • The other 12 clinics
  • Patient text-message reminders
  • Insurance pre-checks
  • Billing

If the pilot passes, year one extends the same setup to all 14 clinics, with the Riverside team training the next ones.
