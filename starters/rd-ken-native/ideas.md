# Ideas
## 1. Hiring-signal shortlist
Starts from: rd-ken-native
Fills: `data.why_now`, `data.search`, `data.shortlist`
Why it gets a reply: the people on the list are the ones the account is hiring for right now.
Point the search at roles the account just posted and use the open role as the reason on each card.
## 2. Three buyer roles
Starts from: rd-ken-native
Fills: `data.audience`, `data.shortlist`, `data.emails`
Why it gets a reply: each email speaks to a different role in the buying group.
Pick two people from each of three roles and write one email per role, so the page shows the account's whole buying group.
## 3. Partner channel list
Starts from: rd-ken-native
Fills: `data.brief`, `data.audience`, `data.draft`
Why it gets a reply: it shows the account who could resell for them, not only who could buy.
Aim the search at agencies or resellers and change the draft email to a partnership ask.
