# Ideas
## 1. Audit readiness check
Starts from: readiness-checklist
Fills: `data.topic`, `data.items`, `data.closing`
Why it gets a reply: the reader has a deadline and sees the gaps before it.
Set the topic to an audit or certification your buyers prepare for and make the first steps free actions.
## 2. Go-live checklist for new customers
Starts from: readiness-checklist
Fills: `data.topic`, `data.headline`, `data.items`
Why it gets a reply: it helps a signed customer launch, which is a reason to talk now.
Send it after the deal closes with items for what the customer must have ready before the first week.
## 3. Team-wide shared link
Starts from: readiness-checklist
Fills: `data.intro`, `data.items`, `data.closing`
Why it gets a reply: several people at the account can tick it, so the page travels inside the company.
Send one link with no person, write the intro for the whole team and pre-tick from the account's own news.
