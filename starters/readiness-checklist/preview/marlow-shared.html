The checklist

10 items, in the order that matters most. We ticked what we could see from outside, so fix anything we got wrong.

Close these first

5 gaps, in the order to close them. Each one comes with a first step you can take this week.

  1. A returns policy customers find in ten seconds

    First stepOpen your site on a phone and time how long it takes to find the refund time. Over ten seconds means a support ticket.

  2. The holiday window set before the peak starts

    First stepPut the date the window changes back in the calendar now, with a reminder two weeks before it.

  3. One place where every return starts

    First stepCount how many lists a return can live on today. More than one means someone reconciles them by hand in January.

  4. Carrier pickup booked from the return itself

    First stepStart a return for a sofa as a customer and count the emails it takes to get a pickup date.

  5. Showrooms that take online orders back

    First stepAsk a showroom manager what they do when a customer walks in with an online order. Write down the steps.

  6. A refund time you commit to and can measure

    First stepPull last January's refunds and measure the time from received to refunded. That is your real number.

  7. Seasonal staff trained on the top return reasons

    First stepPull last year's return reasons, keep the top five, and write one paragraph of guidance for each.

  8. A daily view of returns in transit and their value

    First stepBuild the view from carrier tracking and your order list, even in a spreadsheet, and look at it every day of December.

  9. Rules for opened and damaged items

    First stepWrite the rule for the three items you take back most often. Add the rest as they come up.

  10. Return reasons captured in a fixed list

    First stepReplace the free text reason box with eight options and an other field.

Everything on this list is in place. If that holds up on a closer look, Marlow Home is ready, and the call below is a short one.
