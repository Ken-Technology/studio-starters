# Ideas
## 1. Tool consolidation case
Starts from: roi-calculator
Fills: `data.tool_spend`, `data.offer`, `data.reasons`
Why it gets a reply: the saving is a bill they already pay and can look up.
Lead with the tool spend that goes away and set the share replaced from your Knowledge.
## 2. Savings only, no price
Starts from: roi-calculator
Fills: `data.hours_per_week`, `data.hourly_cost`, `data.team_size`
Why it gets a reply: with no price on the page the reader checks their own cost first.
Leave the seat price out of Knowledge so the page shows gross savings and hours back, and bring pricing to the call.
## 3. Cost of one role's busywork
Starts from: roi-calculator
Fills: `data.work_name`, `data.team_size`, `data.reasons`
Why it gets a reply: it names a task the reader's own team does every week.
Name one repetitive task for one team, start the sliders small and write each reason as a short plain sentence.
