# Ideas
## 1. Hiring manager video
Starts from: role-pitch
Fills: `data.video`, `data.team`, `data.why_you`
Why it gets a reply: hearing the manager say why they want this person is more personal than text.
Add one short video from the hiring manager and keep the written note as backup.
## 2. Passive senior candidates
Starts from: role-pitch
Fills: `data.why_you`, `data.process`, `data.headline`
Why it gets a reply: a senior person in a job needs a short, low-risk first step.
Cut the process to two steps, make the first a confidential 15-minute call and keep the pay range up top.
## 3. Referral from the team
Starts from: role-pitch
Fills: `data.why_you`, `data.team`, `sender.name`
Why it gets a reply: it comes from a colleague, not a recruiter.
Send it under the name of an engineer on the team and write the team note in their voice.
