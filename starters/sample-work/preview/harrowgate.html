What we made

4 parts, in the order to read them. Each one stands on its own, so take what is useful and leave the rest.

  1. The homepage headline

    Rewrite 1

    Next-generation and empower are on every palletizer site, so a plant manager reads past them. The two numbers that decide a visit are further down your own page: 40 cases a minute and a changeover under 10 minutes. The headline should carry them.

    On your site now. Next-generation palletizing solutions that empower manufacturers to optimize throughput and reduce downtime.

    Our version. Palletizing robots for food and beverage lines. Up to 40 cases a minute, and a new pattern in under 10 minutes.

  2. The HX Series paragraph

    Rewrite 2

    The first sentence says nothing a buyer can check. The facts that follow it are good, so the rewrite keeps them and drops the adjectives. Mixed cases on one line is the detail a plant with many SKUs is looking for, so it goes first.

    On your site now. Flexible, scalable, and designed for the demands of modern production environments, the HX Series sets a new standard in robotic palletizing.

    Our version. The HX Series palletizes mixed cases on one line, up to 40 a minute. Change the pattern from the panel and run again in under 10 minutes.

  3. The LineSight paragraph

    Rewrite 3

    Real-time visibility and advanced analytics describe a category, not your product. Your second sentence already says what it does. The rewrite leads with that and names who looks at it, because a line supervisor and a plant manager open it for different reasons.

    On your site now. Real-time visibility into end-of-line performance, powered by advanced analytics. See every stop and its cause, on the floor or on your phone.

    Our version. LineSight shows every stop on the line and what caused it. The supervisor sees it on the floor, and you see it on your phone.

  4. What we would not touch yet

    Left as is

    Two parts of the page are already doing their job, or need something a rewrite cannot give them.

    • The service section24/7 support, parts within 48 hours, technicians in 14 states. Specific and short. Keep it, and move it higher up the page.
    • The customers sectionTrusted by leading manufacturers names nobody. It needs customer interviews first, not new words.
