Customers are seafood processors and frozen food distributors moving through the port. Announced a 20,000 square foot expansion with 4 more dock doors, opening next spring.

What we made

4 parts, in the order to read them. Each one stands on its own, so take what is useful and leave the rest.

  1. How many trailers a day the docks can turn

    The question

    The expansion takes you from 8 doors to 12, so the question is what the docks can take before and after, and whether receiving is the limit or the floor is. This model answers the first part from your door count and your hours. The floor question needs your pallet counts, which are not public.

  2. Three inputs, two from your site

    Inputs

    Two numbers come from your site. The third, the time a trailer spends at a door, is not public, so the model leaves it to you.

    • 8 dock doors today, 12 after the expansionFrom the homepage and the expansion note.
    • Receiving runs 24 hours a dayFrom the homepage.
    • Minutes per trailer at the doorNot on your site. Put in your average for frozen pallets with a check-in and every line below follows from it.

  3. Trailers a day, before and after

    The method

    One door turns 1,440 minutes a day divided by your minutes per trailer. Eight doors turn eight times that and twelve turn twelve times that, so the expansion raises dock capacity by half whatever your door time is. If the floor cannot absorb half again as many trailers a day, the new doors will wait on forklifts, not the other way round.

    • Today: 8 doors times the turns per door1,440 minutes a day, divided by your minutes per trailer, times 8.
    • After the expansion: 12 doors times the turns per doorThe same turns per door, times 12.
    • Every minute saved at the doorRaises the turns of every door at once, so after the expansion it counts 12 times.

  4. Measure the door time before the doors open

    What to change first

    The one number the model cannot get from your site is the time a trailer spends at a door. Measure it for two weeks on the 8 doors you have: time in, time out, frozen or chilled. With that number, the model tells you whether the 4 new doors need more forklifts, or whether a faster check-in gets you most of the way there.
