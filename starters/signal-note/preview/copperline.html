Two things we noticed about Copperline Software this month

For Sam Ortega, Head of Revenue Operations at Copperline Software

Sam,

Each month I write down what changed at a few companies I follow, and what it usually means. This one is about Copperline Software. Two things stood out.

  1. 1 News Seen October 22

    Copperline launched a mobile app for technicians

    Copperline published a post on October 22 launching a mobile app for technicians.1

    What it usually means. A product moves to a new kind of user when it gets an app for them. Support questions usually change first, then pricing questions, and the sales team starts hearing from people who do not run the account.

    One idea. Read the first month of support tickets from technicians and tag each one by cause. Share the top three with sales before the next pricing call.

  2. 2 Role change Seen September 15

    You became Head of Revenue Operations in September

    You joined Copperline as Head of Revenue Operations. A change like that was reported on September 15.2

    What it usually means. A new revenue operations lead usually spends the first quarter on one question: which numbers the team trusts. Tooling choices often follow that answer, not the other way round.

    One idea. Pick one number the sales team and finance report differently, and trace both back to where they start. Fix that one before you add a dashboard.

I put these two together because a new app and a new revenue operations lead tend to need the same thing: one agreed set of numbers about who uses the product. Nothing here needs an answer. If it is useful, I will send another one next month. If it is not, tell me and I will stop.

Jonas
