Three things we noticed about Marlowe Foods this month

For the team at Marlowe Foods

Hello,

Each month I write down what changed at a few companies I follow, and what it usually means. This one is about Marlowe Foods. Three things stood out.

  1. 1 Funding Seen October 20

    Marlowe Foods raised a growth round

    Marlowe Foods announced a growth round from a regional food fund on October 20.1

    What it usually means. New money usually comes with a plan the investor has already seen: more volume, a wider customer list, or both. The team then has a few months to show that the plant and the paperwork can carry it.

    One idea. List the three promises in the funding plan that depend on the plant, and give each one an owner and a date. Review the list monthly.

  2. 2 Expansion Seen October 8

    A second packaging line is coming to the Ohio plant

    Marlowe Foods announced a second packaging line at its Ohio plant on October 8.2

    What it usually means. A second line often exposes what one line hid: changeover time, label checks and the hours a shift lead spends on handoffs. Output tends to rise before the routines around it catch up.

    One idea. Time one changeover on the first line this week and write the steps as a checklist. Use it to set up the second line.

  3. 3 Hiring Seen September 29

    Marlowe Foods is hiring a head of quality

    A head of quality role is open on the Marlowe Foods careers page, seen on September 29.3

    What it usually means. A first head of quality is often hired when retail buyers start asking for audit records, or when the volume is too high for the founders to check by eye.

    One idea. Collect every audit request and customer complaint from the past year in one sheet before the hire starts. It becomes their first week of work.

I put these three together because each one asks more of the plant than it did a quarter ago: more money, more capacity and a new person to hold quality. Nothing here needs an answer. If it is useful, reply and tell me which part. If it is not, tell me and I will stop writing.

Priya
