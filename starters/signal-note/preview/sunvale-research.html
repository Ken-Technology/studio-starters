One thing we noticed about Sunvale Health Clinics this month

For Dana Whitfield, Practice Manager at Sunvale Health Clinics

Dana,

Each month I write down what changed at a few companies I follow, and what it usually means. This one is about Sunvale Health Clinics. One thing stood out.

  1. 1 Note

    Sunvale opened a third clinic in September

    Our research notes say Sunvale Health Clinics opened a third clinic in September.1

    What it usually means. A third site often means the front desk, scheduling and supply ordering that worked for two clinics start to strain. Small differences between sites tend to show up as patient complaints before they show up in a report.

    One idea. Pick the three tasks every clinic does daily and write each one as a half-page checklist. Compare how each site does them this month.

I wrote this one on a single thing, because that was the only change I could back up this month. Nothing here needs an answer. If it is useful, I will send another one next month. If it is not, tell me and I will stop.
