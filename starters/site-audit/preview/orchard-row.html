What I found

Each quote is copied from orchardrow.example as it read when I scored it.

Message clarity

3 of 10
“Payroll that works the way your business does.”Homepage headline

Any business could print this line. A restaurant owner cannot tell that Orchard Row is built for kitchens and shifts, and the pricing page is where that finally shows up.

Proof

4 of 10
“Join the businesses that run payroll with confidence.”Homepage, below the first screen

No business is named and there is no number or review to back it up. A cautious owner reads this as a placeholder.

Call to action

6 of 10
“Start free trial”Main button

A familiar label that works. It does not say how long the trial is or that no card is needed, which are the two questions an owner has before clicking.

Trust

5 of 10
“Orchard Row Payroll LLC, Portland, Oregon”Footer

A company name and a city are all that shows this is a real business. There is no security note, no named team and no About page link in the menu.

Navigation

8 of 10
“Features | Pricing | Restaurants | Sign in”Main menu

Four plain labels in a sensible order, with a page just for restaurants. It is the clearest part of the site, but the restaurant page is not mentioned on the homepage.

Contact path

7 of 10
“Questions? Talk to a payroll expert.”Pricing page

A visitor who wants to talk to someone can, and the link sits right by the prices. The homepage has no such line, so only people who open pricing find it.

Your first screen, marked up

A sketch of the first screen a visitor sees on a laptop, with a note on each part.

  1. HeadlineCould sit on any payroll site. Name restaurants and cafes in the first line, as the pricing page already does.
  2. SublineThe line under it says confidence and ease. Replace it with what the product does in a week: run payroll, track hours, pool tips.
  3. Main buttonStart free trial is fine. Add the length of the trial so the owner knows what the click starts.
  4. NavigationThe menu is the strongest part. Link the Restaurants page from the headline area too, not only from the menu.
  5. Proof rowThe proof row is a sentence with no names. Replace it with real customer names or the number of restaurants you serve.
