How Plinth plugs in

One block per tool we connect to today. What moves, how it is set up, and what changes for the people who use it.

Workday

Plinth

Native, one-way HR and finance

Plinth reads cost centers, departments and manager relationships from Workday, so approval chains follow your org chart and every contract lands on the right cost center.

What moves
Workers, cost centers and supervisory orgs, Workday to Plinth, nightly
Setup
Half a day with a Workday admin, through an integration system user
Docs
docs.plinth.example/workday

What changes for your team

Approval chains follow the org chart you already keep in Workday. A contract on a cost center reaches the right manager, and nobody maintains a second list of approvers.

DocuSign

Plinth

Native, two-way E-signature

A contract leaves Plinth for signature in DocuSign and comes back signed, filed against the vendor, with the signed PDF and the audit trail.

What moves
Envelopes, signer status and signed PDFs, both ways, as they happen
Setup
About 15 minutes with a DocuSign admin
Docs
docs.plinth.example/docusign

What changes for your team

Vendor agreements keep going out through DocuSign. What changes is where they come back: filed against the vendor in Plinth with the audit trail, not in one person's inbox.

Microsoft Teams

Plinth

Native Messaging

Approvals and renewal reminders arrive as Teams messages with the contract summary, and approvers act on them without opening Plinth.

What moves
Approval requests and renewal reminders, both ways, as they happen
Setup
About 20 minutes; a Teams admin approves the Plinth app
Docs
docs.plinth.example/teams

What changes for your team

Approvals and renewal reminders arrive in Teams with the contract summary attached. A department director can approve from the message between meetings.

Okta

Plinth

Native Identity and SSO

People sign in to Plinth with Okta. SCIM creates and removes accounts as people join and leave, and Okta groups map to Plinth roles.

What moves
Users and groups, Okta to Plinth, as they change
Setup
About 30 minutes with an Okta admin, SAML and SCIM
Docs
docs.plinth.example/okta

What changes for your team

Everyone signs in through Okta, and SCIM removes access the day someone leaves. IT adds Plinth as an Okta app and is done.
