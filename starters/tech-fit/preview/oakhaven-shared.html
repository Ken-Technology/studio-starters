How Plinth plugs in

One block per tool we connect to today. What moves, how it is set up, and what changes for the people who use it.

Sage Intacct

Plinth

Native, two-way ERP and accounting

Approved vendors and purchase orders flow from Plinth into Intacct, and posted bills flow back to the contract they belong to.

What moves
Vendors, purchase orders, bills and dimensions, every 30 minutes
Setup
About an hour with an Intacct admin and a Web Services user
Docs
docs.plinth.example/sage-intacct

What changes for your team

A subcontractor approved in Plinth lands in Intacct as a vendor with its purchase order, and posted bills flow back to the subcontract. The project accountant stops keying vendors twice.

DocuSign

Plinth

Native, two-way E-signature

A contract leaves Plinth for signature in DocuSign and comes back signed, filed against the vendor, with the signed PDF and the audit trail.

What moves
Envelopes, signer status and signed PDFs, both ways, as they happen
Setup
About 15 minutes with a DocuSign admin
Docs
docs.plinth.example/docusign

What changes for your team

Subcontract agreements keep going out through DocuSign. They come back signed and filed against the subcontractor in Plinth, with the audit trail, instead of sitting in a project folder.

Ramp

Plinth

Native, two-way Spend management

Vendor card spend in Ramp shows up against the vendor's contract in Plinth, so you see what is contracted next to what is being spent.

What moves
Vendors and transactions, Ramp to Plinth, daily; vendor records, Plinth to Ramp
Setup
About 10 minutes; a Ramp admin signs in once
Docs
docs.plinth.example/ramp

What changes for your team

Field spend on Ramp cards shows up against the vendor's contract in Plinth, so a superintendent's card purchases sit next to what was contracted with that supplier.
