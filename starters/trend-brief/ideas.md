# Ideas
## 1. Hiring market brief
Starts from: trend-brief
Fills: `data.segments`, `data.plan`, `data.counts`
Why it gets a reply: hiring counts show whether competitors are growing or cutting.
Tell the plan step to pick the hiring surge, headcount grew and headcount cut signals, and open the brief for a recruiting or HR audience.
## 2. New money brief
Starts from: trend-brief
Fills: `data.segments`, `data.counts`, `data.offer`
Why it gets a reply: funded and newly founded companies are the ones buying.
Pick the raised money and new entrants signals, and use the offer paragraph for what you sell to a company that just raised.
## 3. Offer-led brief
Starts from: trend-brief
Fills: `data.offer`, `data.so_what`, `knowledge.stable`
Why it gets a reply: the reader sees the market and the product in the same page.
Write your offer into Knowledge in detail so the closing section names where you fit against each of the three trends.
