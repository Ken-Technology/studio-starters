Contractors are staffing up fast for work already won

3,260

Hiring surge, 3 months

8% of 41,800 companies in construction

How we counted

Construction companies that added staff fast in the last three months

From company profiles and hiring data, counted on the day this brief was filled.

What the count says

3,260 of the 41,800 construction companies counted added staff fast in the last three months, about 8 percent of the industry. Hiring at that pace usually follows work that is already signed. So roughly one contractor in twelve is building a team for projects it has booked, and competing for the same people to do it.

What it means for Granite Peak Builders

Granite Peak is part of that 8 percent, with the school district program signed and eleven project engineer roles open. The firms in this count are the ones bidding against you for the same engineers. It is worth deciding now which roles you fill with hires and which scopes you hand to subcontractors, before the next award lands.

A new set of mid-size competitors has arrived

Moved up a size band

How we counted

Construction companies that crossed into a larger headcount band

What the count says

1,490 construction companies crossed into a larger headcount band, about 4 percent of the industry. These firms were one size smaller not long ago and now show up in the next bracket. A move like that changes what they can bond, what they can bid on and which subcontractors they can keep busy.

What it means for Granite Peak Builders

At 320 people Granite Peak sits in the middle of the 201 to 500 band, so firms that just entered it are new competition for mid-size healthcare and education work in Colorado. They need the same self-perform crews and project engineers you do. Knowing which of the 1,490 are in your region would sharpen the next pursuit list.

Outside money is rare, which makes the few deals worth watching

Raised money, 6 months

1% of 41,800 companies in construction

How we counted

Construction companies that announced funding in the last six months

What the count says

370 of the 41,800 construction companies announced funding in the last six months, about 1 percent of the industry. Capital from outside is unusual in construction, so a short list like this names the companies with the means to change a market quickly. Small count, high signal.

What it means for Granite Peak Builders

For Granite Peak this is a watch list rather than a worry. Any of the 370 that moves into Colorado will chase the same school and hospital programs you just won, and can afford to price low in the first year. It is also the list of possible buyers, should that question ever come up for the owners.

Where I would start

Start with the hiring count. It is the largest of the three and it touches Granite Peak this quarter, with eleven engineer roles open against 3,260 other contractors hiring at the same time. The size band and funding counts are worth a look once a quarter, mostly as a list of names to watch in Colorado.
