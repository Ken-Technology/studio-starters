Trend 01

Growth is concentrated in a small group of operators

9,840

How we countedHospitals and health care companies that grew headcount by at least 10 percentKen Search, counted on the day this brief was filled.

What the count says

9,840 of the 112,600 health care companies in Ken Search grew headcount by at least 10 percent, about 9 percent of the industry. The other 91 percent did not grow at that pace. So the growth in this market sits with fewer than one company in ten, and those are the ones adding sites and staff.

What it means for Larkspur Dental Partners

Larkspur is in that 9 percent after adding six practices in a year. The groups in this count are the ones bidding against you for the next practice, and for the office managers and hygienists who run it. It is worth knowing which of them operate in Ohio and Kentucky before the next seller takes offers.

Trend 02

How we countedHospitals and health care companies that cut headcount by at least 10 percentKen Search, counted on the day this brief was filled.

What the count says

4,120 health care companies cut headcount by at least 10 percent, about 4 percent of the industry. That is close to half the size of the group that grew. A cut of that size usually means an operator is deciding which sites to keep, and that is when practices and small groups come up for sale.

What it means for Larkspur Dental Partners

For a group that has bought six practices in twelve months, this count is a sourcing list. Some of the 4,120 own dental practices they would rather sell than restaff. Your new VP of Revenue Cycle will also inherit whatever billing backlog comes with each one, so a plan for the first 90 days after close matters as much as the price.

Trend 03

Fresh capital is backing a small number of groups

How we countedHospitals and health care companies that announced funding in the last six monthsKen Search, counted on the day this brief was filled.

What the count says

2,710 of the 112,600 health care companies announced funding in the last six months, about 2 percent of the industry. Funded operators can move faster on acquisitions than groups paying from cash flow. A count this small still names the buyers most likely to outbid on a practice.

What it means for Larkspur Dental Partners

The dossier does not say how Larkspur funds its acquisitions, but the 2,710 funded groups are the competition that can pay more per practice this year. Deciding early which deals you will walk away from on price keeps the pipeline honest, and keeps the integration team from drowning.

What to do with this

Where I would start

Start with the cuts. 4,120 operators trimming staff is the count most likely to put practices on the market in Ohio and Kentucky this year, and Larkspur has shown it can close six in twelve months. Watch the funded group next, since it sets the price you will be bidding against.
