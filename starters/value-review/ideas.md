# Ideas
## 1. First 90 days review
Starts from: value-review
Fills: `data.rows`, `data.wins`, `data.plan`
Why it gets a reply: a new customer wants proof the rollout is working.
Use one `quarter` row for the first 90 days with months inside it, and let the plan list the next three milestones of the rollout.
## 2. Renewal-first review
Starts from: value-review
Fills: `data.rows`, `data.next_note`, `data.plan`
Why it gets a reply: it puts the renewal date and the value delivered on one page.
Fill the start, review and renewal markers so the term meter shows, and write the closing note as the renewal conversation.
## 3. Expansion review
Starts from: value-review
Fills: `data.adoption_note`, `data.rows`, `data.plan`
Why it gets a reply: teams with unused seats make the case for training or more seats.
Fill team rows with seats assigned against seats active, and let the plan name the teams to onboard next.
