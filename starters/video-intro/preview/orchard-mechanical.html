How Orchard's dispatchers could stop rebuilding the board every morning

Why I made this for Orchard Mechanical

Three things I looked at before writing this, and what I would do about each one.

  • Three dispatcher openings in one quarter

    That is a lot of hiring for a team your size. Most of the time it points to work the board should be doing for the dispatchers, like reshuffling the day when a job runs long.

  • Two branches, one set of technicians

    Your service and install crews run out of both Ohio branches. When each branch keeps its own board, a technician who is free in one branch is invisible to the other.

  • Service and install on the same day

    Around 40 technicians a day across service calls and installs is where a shared spreadsheet starts to slip. I would start with the service side, because that is where a missed callback shows up first.
