Why I made this for Pinecrest Logistics

Three things I looked at before recording this, and what I would do about each one.

  1. A new terminal in Spokane

    Terminal number eleven adds lanes to and from every other site. Most of the planning for those lanes lands on the same dispatchers who already plan the other ten.

  2. 160 trucks across 11 terminals

    At that size, a truck that leaves half empty from one terminal is hard to see from another. I would want to know how your planners spot that today.

  3. Regional and less-than-truckload

    Short lanes with many stops are where load planning pays off most, because one bad stop order shows up in the driver's day right away. This is where I would start.
