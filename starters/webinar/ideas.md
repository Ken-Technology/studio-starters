# Ideas
## 1. Role-specific session
Starts from: webinar
Fills: `data.title`, `data.takeaways`, `data.why_you`
Why it gets a reply: the takeaways match the reader's own job.
Run one session per role, such as finance or operations, and set the title, speakers and agenda for that role.
## 2. Regional edition
Starts from: webinar
Fills: `data.zones`, `data.local_zone`, `data.date_text`
Why it gets a reply: a start time in their own city removes the first doubt.
Pick the three zones of one region, set a time that works for them and let each account see its own city first.
## 3. Recording first
Starts from: webinar
Fills: `data.recording_note`, `data.register_note`, `data.title`
Why it gets a reply: a person who cannot attend live still asks for the recording.
Lead the note and the registration text with the recording and the slides, and keep the live time as a second option.
