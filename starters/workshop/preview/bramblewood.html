What Bramblewood Foods would take home

  • A close calendar for a six-person finance team

    Every step of Bramblewood's month-end on one page, split between the two plants and head office, with the day each step happens and what it waits on.

  • The three steps to fix first

    Scored by days lost and effort to change. With bills from about 250 suppliers, invoice matching will be on the list, but the room decides from your own map.

  • A one-page 90-day plan for your CFO

    What changes in month one, two and three, written so the senior accountant you are hiring can pick it up on day one.
