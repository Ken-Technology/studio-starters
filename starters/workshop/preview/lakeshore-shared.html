A seat held for Lakeshore Dental Partners

Your patient intake, rebuilt in one morning

A hands-on morning for dental groups. You map how a new patient gets from the first call to the chair at your offices, find where the forms repeat, and leave with one intake flow and a rollout order.

To the team at Lakeshore Dental Partners,

Lakeshore opened its ninth office in September, and each office keeps its own front desk and intake forms. The morning is about turning nine versions of intake into one that every office can still run its own way.

Rosa ImbertClinical onboarding lead, Halden Practice Systems

What you leave with

Three things Lakeshore Dental Partners would take home

  1. 01

    One intake flow for all nine offices

    Each step from the first call to the chair, drawn once for the group, with the places an office is allowed to differ marked on it.

  2. 02

    A shortlist of the forms to retire first

    Which of the nine offices' forms ask the same thing twice, and the three to replace in the first month.

  3. 03

    A rollout order for the nine offices

    Which office switches first and why, who signs off at each one, and the checklist a new office would get on day one.
