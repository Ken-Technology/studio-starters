To the team at Lakeshore Dental Partners,

Lakeshore opened its ninth office in September, and each office keeps its own front desk and intake forms. The morning is about turning nine versions of intake into one that every office can still run its own way.

Rosa ImbertClinical onboarding lead, Halden Practice Systems

What Lakeshore Dental Partners would take home

  • One intake flow for all nine offices

    Each step from the first call to the chair, drawn once for the group, with the places an office is allowed to differ marked on it.

  • A shortlist of the forms to retire first

    Which of the nine offices' forms ask the same thing twice, and the three to replace in the first month.

  • A rollout order for the nine offices

    Which office switches first and why, who signs off at each one, and the checklist a new office would get on day one.

The session, hour by hour

  1. 9:00 am

    How the morning works

    20 min, talk

    Cameras on, one shared board, and a breakout room per group.

  2. 9:20 am

    Map your intake as it runs today

    50 min, hands-on

    From the first call to the chair, one office at a time. Mark every form and every handoff.

  3. 10:10 am

    Break

    10 min

  4. 10:20 am

    Find the repeats

    50 min, hands-on

    Lay the offices side by side. Circle the questions asked twice and the steps only one office does.

  5. 11:10 am

    Draw the one flow

    50 min, hands-on

    Agree the shared steps, mark where an office can differ, and pick the forms to retire first.

  6. 12:00 pm

    Rollout order and read-outs

    30 min, talk

    Each group shows its flow. We send the boards and the checklist the same day.

What to bring

  • Your current intake forms, printed or as PDFs, from at least two offices
  • New patient numbers by office for last month
  • A second screen, if you have one
  • One front-desk person from one office, if they can join for the morning

Who it is for

Practice managers, office leads and the people who answer the phones at dental groups with 3 to 20 offices.

Group size

10 people on one call, so everyone's work gets looked at.
