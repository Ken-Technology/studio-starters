Close the books in five days

A hands-on afternoon for finance teams. You map your own month-end close, pick the three steps that cost the most days, and leave with a plan to fix them.

Hi Helene,

It's a small room of people who run month-end at companies like Norland Packaging, and I would like your team's close on the wall next to theirs.

Noor HaddadHead of Customer Education, Ledgerline

What you leave with

Three things Norland Packaging would take home

  1. 01

    A close calendar for your team

    Every step of your month-end on one page, with the day it happens and what it waits on.

  2. 02

    The three steps to fix first

    Scored by days lost and effort to change, with an owner next to each one.

  3. 03

    A one-page 90-day plan

    What changes in month one, two and three, written so you can hand it to your CFO as it is.
