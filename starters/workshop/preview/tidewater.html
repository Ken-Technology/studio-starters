Pick your first automation cell in one day

A full day on the demo floor for fabricators. You bring drawings for the parts you weld most, score them for a robotic cell, and leave with one part picked, a layout sketch and a payback sheet.

Hi Dale,

Tidewater runs welding on two shifts and has four welder positions open this month. The day is built around that gap: which hull or deck parts a cell could take off your welders first, scored from your own drawings.

Idris OkonkwoApplications engineer, Pellam Robotics

What you leave with

Three things Tidewater Marine Fabrication would take home

  1. 01

    One hull or deck part picked for a cell

    Scored from the drawings you bring, by seam length, repeat volume and fixturing, then run on the demo cell so you leave with a measured cycle time.

  2. 02

    A layout sketch for the Wilmington yard

    Where the cell sits, how aluminum sections reach it across two shifts, and who on each shift runs it.

  3. 03

    A payback sheet using your own hours

    Hours by part from your records against the cell cost and the four welder openings, ready to take to whoever signs off.
